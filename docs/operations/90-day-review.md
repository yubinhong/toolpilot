# ToolPilot 90-day operating handoff

The implementation is not evidence of 90 days of operation. Start dates depend on owner-reviewed content and authorized release. No GSC export, analytics account or conversion data was supplied in this session.

## Sequence

| Window | Work | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–2 | Review/indexing foundation, HTTP diagnosis | TASK-005 checks and public audit |
| Weeks 3–4 | Eight evidence packs, templates, trust details | Owner decisions on exact revisions |
| Weeks 5–6 | Comparisons, alternatives, pricing | Independent intent, source/dependency checks |
| Weeks 7–8 | Best pages, guides, links, freshness | First-batch review and maintenance report |
| Weeks 9–10 | Inspect actual GSC, improve existing pages | Latest 28 days vs preceding 28 days |
| Weeks 11–13 | Expand only supported intent clusters | Written rationale per proposed new page |

## Weekly record (copy for each actual review)

- Period and previous comparison period: unknown until export provided.
- Data source/export date/property: unknown.
- Indexable source routes / Google indexed URLs: distinguish these counts.
- Page/query impressions, clicks, CTR, average position: unknown.
- Queries are aggregate public business keywords only; do not commit raw sensitive exports.
- Top gaining/losing pages, likely causes, one proposed change and revision: pending data.
- PV, outbound clicks, revenue and affiliate conversions: unknown; no analytics enabled.
- Freshness queue and restricted links: use maintenance reports, not assumed verification.

Once there are at least 30 genuinely useful indexed pages, if the latest 28 days still have only tens of impressions with four consecutive flat weeks, pause expansion and review intent, quality, topic boundaries and competition. Do not delete individual URLs on short-term noise.

## Experiments and commercial gates

Change one major title or verdict variable at a time, retain period and revision, and treat low-volume results as directional. No automated A/B infrastructure is added. Do not equate outbound clicks with conversions.

Ads/affiliate activation requires confirmed relationship terms, disclosure, attribution, privacy/consent and operator details. GSC property access, external messages and commercial applications require their own authorization. MCP/Chinese/calculators remain out of scope until a new approved specification.
